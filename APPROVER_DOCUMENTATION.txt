              TIME OFF MODULE - APPROVER USER MANUAL
           (Reviewing Pending Requests & Approval Email Examples)
================================================================================

1. EMAIL NOTIFICATION: REVIEWING NEW LEAVE REQUESTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Check your email inbox:
   - When an employee submits a leave request, you will receive an automated 
     email: "Time Off Request: [Employee Name]".
   - The email contains a request summary:
     * Employee Name
     * Leave Type (e.g. Paid Time Off, Sick Leave)
     * From Date and To Date
     * Duration (e.g. 2 days 4 hours)
     * Description / Reason
2. Click the "View Request ->" button inside the email to open the request 
   directly in Odoo.

[IMAGE PLACEHOLDER: Email Notification Sample - Request Pending Approval]


2. ACCESSING PENDING REQUESTS IN ODOO
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Open the "Time Off" app from the main menu.
2. Navigate to "Management" -> "Time Off" (or Approvals).
3. Filter the list by "To Approve" or "Second Approval Needed".
4. Click on any request to open and review it.

[IMAGE PLACEHOLDER: Time Off Approvals List - Pending Requests]


3. REVIEWING LEAVE REQUEST DETAILS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
On the leave request form, verify the following:
- Employee Name: Confirms who requested the leave.
- Time Off Type: Ensures the correct leave category is selected.
- Dates & Duration: Displays the exact date range and calculated duration.
- Description: Notes or reason entered by the employee.
- Available Balance: Verifies the employee has enough remaining leave days/hours.

[IMAGE PLACEHOLDER: Leave Request Form - Reviewing Details]


4. TAKING ACTION: APPROVING OR REFUSING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. To Approve:
   - Click "Approve" (or "Validate") in the top action bar.
   - Single Approval: The request is confirmed and approved. The employee receives 
     an automated confirmation email ("Time Off Approved").
   - Two-Level Approval: The direct manager clicks "Approve" -> Status moves to 
     "Second Approval Needed". The HR Officer receives an email notification 
     and clicks "Validate" for final approval.

2. To Refuse (Reject):
   - Click "Refuse" in the top action bar.
   - Status changes to "Refused". The employee receives an automated email 
     notification ("Time Off Refused").

[IMAGE PLACEHOLDER: Action Buttons - Approve / Validate / Refuse]


5. TWO-STEP APPROVAL & EMAIL NOTIFICATIONS SUMMARY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- First Approver Email: Sent to direct manager upon employee submission.
- Second Approver Email: Sent to HR Officer once the first manager approves.
- Employee Confirmation Email: Sent to employee upon final approval or refusal.
