1. HOW TO VIEW YOUR LEAVE BALANCE & DASHBOARD
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Click the App Menu in the top-left and select "Time Off".
2. On your dashboard, check your leave cards:
   - Available Balance: Shows your balance in days and hours (e.g. 4 days 4 hours).
   - Expiration Notice: Shows carry-forward leaves and expiry date (e.g. 3 days valid until 30-04-2027).
   - Details: Click the info icon on the card to see the breakdown of your allocations.

[IMAGE PLACEHOLDER: Time Off Dashboard and Leave Cards]


2. HOW TO REQUEST / SUBMIT A LEAVE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. From the Time Off dashboard, click "New" (or click and drag on the calendar).
2. Select "Time Off Type" (e.g. Paid Time Off, Sick Leave).
3. Select the dates:
   - Choose "From" and "To" dates.
   - For half days, select "Half Day" (Morning / Afternoon).
4. Enter a reason in the "Description" field.
5. Click "Save" to submit your request.

[IMAGE PLACEHOLDER: New Time Off Request Form]


3. HOW TO TRACK YOUR REQUEST STATUS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Go to "My Time Off" -> "My Requests" (or check your dashboard calendar).
2. Check the status column:
   - To Approve: Waiting for your manager to review.
   - Second Approval: Approved by manager, waiting for HR validation.
   - Approved: Your leave is approved and confirmed.
   - Refused: Your request was rejected.

[IMAGE PLACEHOLDER: My Requests List View]


4. EMAIL NOTIFICATIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- When your request is approved, you will receive an email: "Time Off Approved".
- When your request is refused, you will receive an email: "Time Off Refused".

[IMAGE PLACEHOLDER: Approval and Refusal Email Samples]


5. HOW TO EDIT OR CANCEL A REQUEST
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Before Approval (Status: "To Approve"):
  Click on your request, edit the dates/details, or click "Delete".

- After Approval (Status: "Approved"):
  Contact your manager or HR department to cancel or modify an approved leave.
